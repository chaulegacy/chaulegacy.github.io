How an Audit Works
Portfolio Review & Audit

Is your active policy truly performing the way you were told?

Many people discover during an audit that their current coverage is smaller, more temporary, or carries significantly more hidden structural traps than they were led to believe. A clinical review takes the guesswork out, and I will tell you honestly if what you currently own is already an ideal fit.

Results depend on your age, health status, and the specific terms of the policy you hold. No premium modifications or coverage increases can be guaranteed until officially underwritten.

  • Identify your true structural class. Uncover exactly what you own—whether it is a true term policy, a restrictive accidental-death-only gimmick, or a graded-benefit trap.
  • Secure coverage maximization & rate minimization. Review updated market parameters to verify if you qualify for significantly more coverage at your exact current cost, or identical coverage thresholds for less monthly outlay.
  • Tax-free portfolio consolidation. Streamline multiple scattered or fragmented policies into a singular, highly efficient asset dashboard to simplify your household finances.
  • Legacy equity extraction. Check mature permanent policies for hidden, accrued cash surrender value. In many cases, we can leverage those existing funds via tax-free distributions to deploy larger coverage limits with zero out-of-pocket costs.
  • Lock in expiring conversion rights. Understand your absolute deadline parameters before a term policy ends, including hidden conversion privileges that let you upgrade to permanent, cash-building assets with no new medical exams.
